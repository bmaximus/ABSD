# Lessons ledger — where every ABSD rule comes from

Every playbook rule ends with `Provenance: LL-n (…)` or `Provenance: industry baseline`. This ledger is the other end
of that link: what happened, why it slipped, what the kit changed. Entries are permanent (never renumbered); a lesson
that is later contradicted gets a new entry that adds a condition (`absd/playbook/process/enrichment.md`).

Types: **escape** (reached a user or the live system) · **finding** (caught by an audit, CI or an exploratory session
before users) · **process** (how the pipeline itself failed) · **practice** (a technique that worked and became a rule).
